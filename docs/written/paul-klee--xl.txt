22 sts and 34 rounds to 10cm on 3mm needles, ribbing on 2.75mm, colourwork on 3.75mm.

Neck (yoke)
  Cast on 118 in MC. (118 sts) [rib]
  Join to work in the round.
  20 rounds of k1, p1 in MC. [rib]

Back neck (yoke)
  Change to the main needles.
  Shaping round in MC: [k6, kfb] x 2, [k8, kfb] x 10, [k6, kfb] x 2, to 132 sts.
  German short rows in MC: 7 pairs, the first 5 sts past the beginning of the round, each 4 further, the last 5 further again. Last row back: [pfb, p4] x 5, pfb, p3. Row to the end of the round: [kfb, k4] x 5, kfb, k3. (144 sts)

(272 sts)
  Knit on to the new beginning of the round, in MC.
  75 rounds of k1 in MC.
  Change to the ribbing needles.
  2 rounds of k1 in MC. [rib]
  31 rounds of k1, p1 in MC. [rib]
  Bind off in pattern, (k1, p1), in MC. [rib]
