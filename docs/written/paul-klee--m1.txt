22 sts and 34 rounds to 10cm on 3mm needles, ribbing on 2.75mm, colourwork on 3.75mm.

Neck (yoke)
  Cast on 106 in MC. (106 sts) [rib]
  Join to work in the round.
  20 rounds of k1, p1 in MC. [rib]

Back neck (yoke)
  Change to the main needles.
  Shaping round in MC: k2, kfb, k5, kfb, [k7, kfb] x 11, k5, kfb, k3, to 120 sts.
  German short rows in MC: 6 pairs, the first 5 sts past the beginning of the round, each 4 further. Last row back: p5, pfb, [p4, pfb] x 3, p3. Row to the end of the round: k5, kfb, [k4, kfb] x 3, k3. (128 sts)
