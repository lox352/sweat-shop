Paul Klee Sweater, size Kids 4
22 sts and 34 rounds to 10cm on 3mm needles, ribbing on 2.75mm, colourwork on 3.75mm.

Neck (yoke)
  Cast on 96 in MC. (96 sts) [rib]
  Join to work in the round.
  18 rounds of k1, p1 in MC. [rib]

Back neck (yoke)
  Change to the main needles.
  Shaping round in MC: k3, kfb, k8, kfb, [k9, kfb] x 7, k8, kfb, k4, to 106 sts.
  German short rows in MC: 5 pairs, the first 5 sts past the beginning of the round, each 4 further. Last row back: p2, pfb, [p6, pfb] x 2, p3. Row to the end of the round: k2, kfb, [k6, kfb] x 2, k3. (112 sts)

  Chart colour-1-k1, rows 1-5. (128 sts)
  Chart colour-2-k2, rows 1-5. (160 sts)
  Chart colour-3-plain, rows 1-5. (160 sts)
  Chart colour-4-k4, rows 1-4.
  Chart colour-4-k4, rows 5-5. Body (body)
  Divide, in MC: k44, remove marker for the right back raglan line, wrap the next stitch, then put 60 on hold for sleeve-right, remove marker for the right front raglan line, cast on 3, place marker for the new beginning of the round (the new beginning of the round), cast on 3, cast on 1 more, slip 1 and pass the cast-on over, k88, remove marker for the left front raglan line, wrap the next stitch, then put 60 on hold for sleeve-left, remove marker for the left back raglan line, cast on 6, cast on 1 more, slip 1 and pass the cast-on over, k44. (188 sts)
  Knit on to the new beginning of the round, in MC.
  56 rounds of k1 in MC.
  Change to the ribbing needles.
  2 rounds of k1 in MC. [rib]
  26 rounds of k1, p1 in MC. [rib]
  Bind off in pattern, (k1, p1), in MC. [rib]
