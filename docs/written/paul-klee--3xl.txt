  Cast on 126 in MC. (126 sts) [rib]

  20 rounds of k1, p1 in MC. [rib]

  Shaping round in MC: k4, kfb, k8, kfb, [k10, kfb] x 9, k8, kfb, k4, to 138 sts.

Body (body)
  Divide, in MC: k70, remove marker for the right back raglan line, wrap the next stitch, then put 92 on hold for sleeve-right, remove marker for the right front raglan line, cast on 6, place marker for the new beginning of the round (the new beginning of the round), cast on 6, cast on 1 more, slip 1 and pass the cast-on over, k140, remove marker for the left front raglan line, wrap the next stitch, then put 92 on hold for sleeve-left, remove marker for the left back raglan line, cast on 12, cast on 1 more, slip 1 and pass the cast-on over, k70. (304 sts)
  Knit on to the new beginning of the round, in MC.
  75 rounds of k1 in MC.
  Change to the ribbing needles.
  2 rounds of k1 in MC. [rib]
  31 rounds of k1, p1 in MC. [rib]
  Bind off in pattern, (k1, p1), in MC. [rib]
